06 / QUESTIONS

A few things
to know.

Do I need an account to use SessionStream?

You can stream with SessionStream Free without creating an account. Sign in if you want to see your usage and manage connected computers. An account is required for Pro.

Do my listeners need to install anything?

No. They open your listening link in a supported browser and press Start listening. They can listen on a computer, phone, or tablet.

What will my listeners hear?

They hear the audio passing through the SessionStream plugin. Put it on your master bus to share the full mix, or on a track or bus to share just that sound. Changes you make during playback are heard in the stream.

Which DAWs can I use it with?

SessionStream works in compatible VST3 hosts on Windows and Apple Silicon Macs, with AU also available on macOS. This includes DAWs such as Ableton Live, Logic Pro, REAPER, FL Studio, Cubase, and Studio One. Pro Tools / AAX is not supported.

Can we talk while listening?

Yes—use your usual voice or video call alongside SessionStream. SessionStream carries your DAW audio; the separate call handles your conversation. Live audio has normal network delay.
