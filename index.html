Live or buffered audio?

Live audio

Lower-delay monitoring over WebRTC. Delay depends on the network, device and settings.

Buffered audio

Lossless PCM with more bandwidth and additional buffering. Available when the connection needs it.

Questions.

Do I need an account to send audio?+

No account is required to start a Free session with up to three listeners. Pro requires an account for your subscription and additional features.

Can we talk while listening?+

Use your usual voice or video call alongside SessionStream. The plugin carries the audio from your DAW; it does not replace your conversation app.

Does it change my DAW’s output?+

Stream gain changes the outgoing copy. Your DAW level stays unchanged unless you choose Mute local audio. Offline renders aren’t broadcast.

How much delay is there?+

It depends on your connection, the selected transport, and the listener’s device. Live audio has less delay. Buffered PCM adds buffering and can help on restrictive networks.

What happens if I close the plugin window?+

The session continues while the plugin is enabled. Pause keeps the invite available. End session closes the invite. Disabling or removing the plugin releases its session.

How does DAW-to-DAW Receive work?+

Pro users can add SessionStream to a receiving track and enter the sender’s invite. Choose Live WebRTC or Lossless PCM, then listen or record using the receiving DAW.

Do listeners need an account?+

Usually no. Open the invite and start listening. A Pro host can choose to approve each listener or require a signed-in account.
