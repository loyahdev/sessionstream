How do cloud listener-hours work?

One listener-hour is one guest listening for one hour. 300 listener-hours cover two 90-minute classes with 100 listeners, or 60 hours with five listeners. Pro Cloud has no fixed listener cap. Cloud streaming and additional top-ups require an active Pro subscription. Unused top-up hours are retained if you cancel, and become usable again when Pro is active.

Questions.

Do I need an account to send audio?+

No account is required to stream a session with up to three listeners.

Can I share unreleased music?+

Cloud audio uses end-to-end encryption by default, for Pro Cloud sessions. Hosts can switch that layer off in Settings, and the listener page shows which protection is active. Every session has a private invite with an optional passcode. SessionStream does not save a recording library, but invited listeners can record what they hear. Only share confidential work with people you trust. Read the privacy policy.

Can we talk while listening?+

SessionStream shares your DAW's audio while you can use any call or streaming app alongside it with your clients.

Does it change my DAW’s output?+

Your DAW audio is not modified through the plugin. It remains in your DAW as it was before at the same volume and quality you had before.

How much delay is there?+

Expect a delay of a few hundred milliseconds to a few seconds, depending on your connection and the selected transport method.

What happens if I close the plugin window?+

The session continues while the plugin is enabled, and you can pause the session to temporarily stop the audio. Disabling or removing the plugin fully disables its session.

How does DAW-to-DAW Receive work?+

Pro users can add SessionStream to a track and enter the sender’s invite link. Choose Live WebRTC or Lossless PCM, then listen or record using the receiving DAW.

Do listeners need an account?+

No. Open the invite and start listening. Though, a Pro host can choose to approve each listener or require a signed-in account.
