1. Who is responsible

SessionStream operates from Canada and is responsible for handling personal information as described in this policy. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, or account and data deletion, email support@sessionstream.app.

2. Information we handle

  • Accounts: your Clerk account identifier, name, email address, profile photo, and information needed to verify sign-in.
  • Connected computers: installation identifier, computer display name (which may default to its hostname), operating system, plugin version, last-seen time, installation-credential hash, and revocation state.
  • Account-linked usage: streaming duration, combined listener duration, estimated audio bytes, daily totals, and temporary active-session and report identifiers. Actual reporting depends on the plugin version and whether you are signed in.
  • Billing: Clerk billing and Stripe payment identifiers, plan, payment status, renewal information, and records needed for billing and support. Stripe collects payment details; the SessionStream account database does not store full card numbers or security codes.
  • Service operation: information such as IP addresses, browser/device information, request times, error records, and session-link or tunnel information needed by us and our providers to deliver and secure the service.
  • Support: messages, contact information, and any diagnostic information you choose to send.

3. Audio and shared sessions

Audio must be transmitted and is temporarily held in playback or receiving buffers to provide streaming or DAW-to-DAW receiving. These working buffers are not a saved recording library; they are consumed or replaced during playback and cleared as the connection or process ends. Audio is not stored in the account usage database or recorded for usage metrics. Direct sharing sends audio from the producer’s computer. Cloud sharing sends one live stream through our relay provider for distribution. Cloud end-to-end encryption is on by default, with audio keys exchanged separately between the host and approved listener endpoints. Hosts can disable that layer before starting an invitation; the listener page identifies the active protection. Connection encryption remains enabled. When the extra layer is disabled, relay providers can process the encoded audio carried by those connections. We do not promise that all traffic stays within Canada or that all transport is end-to-end encrypted. People with access to a listening session can hear its audio and may record it using their own tools. A collaborator can deliberately record received audio in their DAW. Do not share sensitive or confidential audio with people you do not trust.

4. Why we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts; connect devices and enforce plan/session limits; display usage; process subscriptions and refunds; deliver invitation links and streams; prevent abuse; investigate errors; respond to support and privacy requests; and meet legal obligations. Account-free streaming does not require a SessionStream profile, but connection and security information can still be processed. We do not sell personal information or use the account usage counters for advertising.

5. Providers and processing outside Canada

We use Clerk for authentication, profile and subscription management, Cloudflare for hosting, account storage, network routing and security, and Stripe for payment processing. The website loads fonts through Google Fonts, and profile photos may load from Clerk or a connected-account image provider. Those requests disclose the technical information needed to serve them, such as your IP address and browser headers. Providers may process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws and lawful access requirements of those places. Providers also handle some information under their own policies. We may disclose information when legally required or reasonably needed to protect users and the service; access is otherwise limited to service delivery and the purposes described here.

6. Cookies and local storage

Sign-in and security features use cookies and similar storage. SessionStream’s own web-account cookie is Secure and HttpOnly and normally expires after seven days; Clerk manages additional authentication cookies and tokens. The website saves your theme preference in local storage. The plugin stores sign-in and installation credentials in the operating system’s credential store where supported. Blocking or clearing required storage can sign you out or prevent account features from working. The current website does not include an advertising tracker or a separate behavioural analytics service.

7. Retention and safeguards

Account and device records are kept while needed to provide your account and manage connected installations. The active account database retains daily usage for up to 365 days, short-term report counters for approximately two days, and operational account audit events for approximately 30 days. Temporary sender leases expire after inactivity. Sign-in records expire or are revoked according to the applicable session or provider settings. Billing, support, security, backup, and historical migration records may be retained separately for legal, recovery, dispute, or legitimate recordkeeping needs; they do not necessarily follow the usage-counter schedule. When a verified account-deletion request is completed, we delete or anonymize information no longer needed and request relevant provider-side deletion where applicable. Limited records may remain for the purposes above; backup copies may persist until they expire or are replaced and are not used as an active account. We will explain any necessary exceptions when responding to your request. We use access controls, account isolation, credential hashing and provider security features to reduce risk. No system or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and requests

You can edit profile information through account settings, disconnect computers in the dashboard, cancel subscription renewal through Billing, sign out, or use account-free features where available. You may request access to or correction of your personal information, ask about our practices and service providers, withdraw consent where applicable, or request deletion by emailing support. Requests are handled manually. We may need to verify your identity and explain any records we must retain or services we can no longer provide after a request. For deletion, we also confirm how your active subscription will be closed so future renewals stop. You may contact SessionStream at the support email above about our service-provider practices, including processing outside Canada. We will respond in accordance with applicable law. You may also raise a privacy concern with the privacy authority with jurisdiction.

9. Age requirements and updates

Accounts and Pro purchases are intended for people who have reached the age of majority where they live. SessionStream is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an account, contact support. We may update this policy to reflect service changes or legal requirements, identify the update date, and give appropriate notice of material changes. New purposes requiring consent will be addressed before the information is used for those purposes.
