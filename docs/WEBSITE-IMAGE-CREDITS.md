# Website photography

The images are real photographs downloaded from Pexels, optimized to WebP and served locally. They illustrate music production; the people and equipment pictured are not represented as SessionStream users or endorsers.

- `websrc/assets/studio-session.webp`: Brett Sayles, black-and-white control room. Source: https://www.pexels.com/photo/sound-engineer-creating-musical-arrangement-on-computer-in-recording-studio-4105576/
- `websrc/assets/studio-hands.webp`: TStudio, hands working with a keyboard and sampler. Source: https://www.pexels.com/photo/sounds-equipment-used-in-studio-7173393/
- License checked November 18, 2026: https://www.pexels.com/license/

Original photos are unaltered apart from image compression; layout cropping and reduced saturation on the detail photograph are applied in CSS. The homepage footer credits the two photographs currently displayed, with direct links to their Pexels source pages. Unused assets below are retained for provenance.
