Ready to share

Open or restart your DAW and rescan plugins if SessionStream does not appear.

Use Audio Units (AU) in Logic Pro. In Ableton Live or FL Studio, choose VST3 or AU and enable the corresponding macOS plugin folder.

Add SessionStream to the track you want to share, then:

  1. Optionally enable Use passcode, then click Generate share link and wait for your invite.
  2. Copy the link or display it with Generate QR code. Share any passcode separately.
  3. Click Start streaming.

Your listener opens the link and clicks Start listening. Keep your Mac awake and online while streaming.

Disable/bypass or remove SessionStream to release its session. The engine and tunnel shut down when no enabled SessionStream instances remain in use. Re-enable and generate a new invite to reconnect your listener.
