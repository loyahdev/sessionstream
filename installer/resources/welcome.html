SessionStream 0.10

Share audio from your DAW with a private browser link.

Includes VST3 and Audio Units (AU), designed for compatible macOS DAWs such as Ableton Live, Logic Pro and FL Studio. Use AU in Logic Pro.

Both formats include the streaming engine and Cloudflare tunnel. No Cloudflare account, separate app, Node installation or listener download is needed.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 13.5 or newer, with your DAW running natively. Save your project and close your DAW before updating.

Installs into your account's ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components folders.
